Riviera Made · design system, third direction

Caribbean, cute, and high contrast.

Two earlier tries were wrong in opposite directions. The print-shop one read as workwear; the quiet one read as an art gallery and needed photographs it does not have. This one is warm, sunny and friendly — the register a couple planning a beach wedding actually wants — with enough contrast that it never turns into pastel mush.

One product

The welcome bag. Not four pieces listed like a catalogue — one bag, and a sentence about what is inside it. Every page says the same thing.

No forms

Every call to action opens WhatsApp. It is how the studio already works, it needs no server, and a message gets answered faster than a form ever would.

Contrast on purpose

Dark teal grounds under bright shapes, and dark ink text on every bright fill. Nothing light-on-light, nothing that goes grey in the sun.

The contrast rule: text is always ink #16323A on the bright colours, and sand on deep sea. Never white on coral, never coral on sand as text, never two brights touching without ink between them. That single rule is what keeps it cheerful instead of childish.

Shapes
Pill

The arch holds every photograph — a doorway, which is what this coast is built out of. Cards are softly rounded, buttons are full pills. Nothing is a sharp rectangle and nothing is a plain rounded box with a coloured bar down the side.

Voice

How it sounds

Warm and plain, like a message from someone you like. Contractions are fine. Say the useful thing and stop. "No catalogue, no upsells, no packages with silly names." "We'll draw your bag and send it to you."

The most expensive-sounding fact is true and stays: one wedding a week.
