Things people ask us

Could we just order these online?

You could, and for some weddings that's the right call. Once you add the printed bits from a second shop, a checked bag to bring it down and an evening putting them together in your room, it usually lands within a few dollars of ours.

Is there a minimum?

None. Twelve bags or a hundred and twenty. Under twenty-five is a little more each.

Who puts them in the rooms?

The hotel does, at check-in. A few resorts charge a small fee per bag, and we'll tell you if yours is one of them.

Where do you deliver?

Puerto Morelos, Cancún, Playa Mujeres, Playa del Carmen, Xcaret, Akumal and Tulum. We don't ship anywhere at all.

Send us a message and tell us the date.

No forms, no call. Write to us the way you'd write to a friend — the date, the venue, roughly how many bags — and one of us answers with a picture of your bag.
