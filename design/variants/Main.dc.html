IWhat we make

The welcome bag. Nothing else.

A cotton tote carrying your names and your date, and inside it the weekend printed on a card, two bottle labels and a luggage tag. Every piece is cut and printed by the two of us, for your wedding and no one else's.

There is no catalogue and there are no packages with silly names. We take one wedding a week, and when a week is taken we say so early rather than take the order and hope.

One wedding a week — so yours is the only one on the table.

Two sisters, one room in Puerto Morelos, twenty minutes from most of the resorts on this coast.

IIWhat we take off your hands

It never clears customs

Everything ordered from home crosses a border, and the one person who can chase it is in another country. Ours is made twenty minutes from where you will be standing.

Your count can move

Cousins book late and someone always drops out. Tell us the final number ten days before the wedding; anything unused comes back to us rather than onto your bill.

It is waiting when they land

Delivered to your resort three days ahead, assembled, with a signed receipt. The staff place them in the rooms before your guests arrive, and nothing at all travels in your suitcase.
