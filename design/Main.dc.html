Things people ask us

Could we just order these online?

You could, and for some weddings that's the right call. Once you add the printed bits from a second shop, a checked bag to bring it all down and an evening putting them together in your room, it usually lands within a few dollars of ours — with your count fixed weeks earlier.

Is there a minimum?

None. Twelve bags or a hundred and twenty. Under twenty-five is a little more each, because drawing it is the same work either way.

How late is too late?

Four to six weeks is comfy, and we've done ten days more than once. Ask even if you're sure you've left it too long.

Who puts them in the rooms?

The hotel does, at check-in. A few resorts charge a small fee per bag for that, and we'll tell you if yours is one of them.

Can you match our colours?

Send the palette from your invitations, or just a photo of the fabric. You'll see it on the drawing before anything is cut.

Where do you deliver?

Puerto Morelos, Cancún, Playa Mujeres, Playa del Carmen, Xcaret, Akumal and Tulum. We don't ship anywhere at all — if your wedding is elsewhere we'll say so straight away.

Send us a message and tell us the date.

No forms, no call, no sequence of emails. Write to us the way you'd write to a friend — the date, the venue, roughly how many bags — and one of us answers with a picture of your bag, usually the same afternoon.
