# The PayPal invoice, written out

Paste this into PayPal once, save it as a template, and from then on duplicate
the last invoice and change the four things in [BRACKETS].

Everything here matches /terms on the site. If you change one, change the other,
because a customer who finds a difference is right and you are wrong.

## The invoice itself

**Item name**

    Welcome bags — deposit to hold [DATE]

**Item description**

    [N] welcome bags at $[PRICE] USD each. A cotton tote printed with your names
    and your date, holding a card with your weekend on it, two bottle labels and
    a luggage tag. Made in Puerto Morelos and delivered to [VENUE], assembled,
    on [DELIVERY DATE] — three days before you marry.

**Amount** — half the order. The other half is due when you hand the bags over.

**Note to customer**

    Thank you — this holds your week, and we have taken it off the calendar.

    Nothing is printed until you have approved the drawing, and you already have.
    Your final count is due ten days before the wedding: until then the number can
    move either way, and anything we have not made comes off your final bill.

    The balance is due on delivery, by card, transfer or cash — whichever suits you.

**Terms and conditions**

    50% now, balance on delivery. Prices are in US dollars and delivery within our
    area is included.

    Final guest count is due 10 days before the wedding. After that we will do what
    we can with an increase but cannot promise it; a decrease comes off your bill
    unless the bags are already printed with your names.

    If the wedding moves, tell us — if we still have the new week, the order moves
    with it at no extra cost. If you cancel, you owe nothing beyond the deposit for
    anything we have not yet made; bags already pressed carry your names, cannot go
    to anyone else, and are charged.

    If we misspell a name, print the wrong date, or deliver a damaged bag, tell us
    within three days with a photograph and we will remake it or refund it.

**Memo (private — only you see this)**

    [VENUE] · [DATE] · [N] bags · deposit · balance $[X] on delivery
